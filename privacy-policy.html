Privacy Policy

Effective 16 September 2026

Rozazag is a movie collection and watch-journal app. It does not operate a Rozazag account server and does not sync your library to our computers.

Data stored on your device

Collections, titles, posters, watch logs, watchlist rows, collection-card JPEGs, purchase records, and Card Credit balances stay in the app sandbox (local JSON files, App Storage, and private files). Removing the app or using Clear All Local Data deletes this information. Consumable credits cannot be restored after that. A new session includes 20 local starter Card Credits, which are not an Apple product.

Permissions

  • Camera — only after you tap Take Poster Photo, to capture a still for a title.
  • Photos — only after you tap Choose Poster (read) or Save to Photos (write a collection-card JPEG).
  • Microphone and speech recognition — only after you tap Dictate Review, to fill the written review field. You can type instead.
  • Tracking — the system App Tracking Transparency prompt may appear after you open Collections, Journal, Watchlist, Cards, or Settings. If you allow tracking, Rozazag may read the advertising identifier to attribute Card Credits offers. Rozazag does not sell your posters or photos for ads.
  • Notifications — the system notification prompt may appear after the tracking prompt on those same tabs. If you allow notifications, Rozazag registers for remote notifications and stores the device token on this device.

In-app purchases

Card Credits are Apple In-App Purchase consumables. Apple processes payment. Generating a collection card spends 10 Card Credits after you confirm. Preview is free. Credits live on this device and are not restorable after deletion. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Discover rows are stills bundled on this device, not a live catalog or social network.
