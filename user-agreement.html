By using Rozazag you agree to keep titles, images, and reviews you add. You are responsible for having the rights to posters you capture or import.

The service

Rozazag lets you build collections, log watches, and print a collection card on this device. There is no video streaming and no Rozazag cloud library.

Credits

Generating a collection card spends 10 Card Credits after you confirm. Preview is free. The card still includes every poster in that collection. Apple charges for credit packs. Credits are consumable and are not restored after you delete the app or tap Clear All Local Data. A new session includes 20 local starter Card Credits. Packs are not restored.

Bundled stills

Discover titles are stills included with the app.

Session

There is no Sign in with Apple and no password. After you agree to this User Agreement and the Privacy Policy, you tap Build a Movie Collection. The library stays on this device. Clear All Local Data permanently deletes collections, media, cards, watch history, purchase records, and remaining credits on this device.
